# LinkedIn Post Prompt

Write a LinkedIn post in Spanish announcing {ProjectName} ({status}).

- First line: the problem it solves, in plain words, for the target user.
- Three to five short paragraphs: what it does, what is different, the constraint it respects (privacy, free, local).
- Mention the stack in one line: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Motion, Vercel.
- End with the URL and an invitation to try it and give feedback.
- No emoji walls, no hashtags beyond 3 to 5 at the end, no marketing superlatives.

Cover image: 1584×396, dark slate background with the dot grid, logo and brand name with the accent word in turquoise, Beta badge, and a screenshot of the app on the right.
